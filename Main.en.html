Common patient concerns, answered

Travel for treatment is a major decision. We provide answers before you have to ask.

Are the doctors verified, and how can I check?
Each doctor has a profile listing their name, qualifications and hospital. Credentials are published only after verification.
What if my stay is extended or the plan changes?
Your quotation includes written terms explaining what is covered and what happens if your stay is extended or the plan changes after examination.
Who do I contact if I need help?
You have one named coordinator and a WhatsApp contact who supports you in Egypt and after you return.
Could commercial interests affect medical decisions?
No. Your treating doctor determines the medical plan. We disclose our commercial relationships with hospitals in each quotation.

FAQs

Do I need to travel to receive an initial opinion?
No. You can submit reports through a secure portal and receive written feedback in your home country.
Who can see my medical file and passport?
Only authorized staff. Access is logged. We request a passport only when travel arrangements require it.
Can a family member accompany me?
Yes. Your companion can have an account with permissions you define, and cannot access your medical file without appropriate authorization.
Disclosure of our hospital relationships

We work with the hospitals listed on this website and may have commercial relationships with them. Medical decisions are made independently by the treating doctor and are not influenced by commercial arrangements. Options in quotations are listed alphabetically or according to disclosed criteria.
